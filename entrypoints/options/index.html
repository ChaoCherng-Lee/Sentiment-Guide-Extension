Share with a parent

Only if you want to: a parent can see a summary of how you're doing on their dashboard. Here's exactly what that means.

What your parent will see

  • How many of your messages were flagged each week, and whether yellow or red
  • What kind of problem it was, like "sarcasm" or "harsh tone"
  • How often you took a suggestion
  • Which app: WhatsApp, Instagram or Gmail

What they won't see

  • Your messages, the flagged words or the suggested rewrites
  • Who you were talking to
  • Anything other people sent you

You stay in control

  • "Shared with parent: on" shows in the extension menu while it's on
  • Unlink and delete my data (here) stops sharing and deletes everything shared
  • Anything shared is deleted after 90 days anyway

Shared with parent: on. Your parent sees counts and categories only — never your messages.

Switching a site off removes the permission to read that site. Whatever the site, your messages are only ever sent to the AI running on this computer.
